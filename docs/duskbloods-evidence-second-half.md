## 6. Translation for BloodLocks — design inferences

These recommendations follow the visible examples; they are **not reconstructed internal implementation**.

1. Treat the map as one visual system with explicit modes: browse information, start-phase region choice, and death revival selection. Every mode needs its own heading, confirm rules and valid destinations. A generic unlabeled map is insufficient.
2. Use authored encounter groups tied to terrain: a wide street for cavalry, a clear landing for a heavy, a nave lane for a mixed pack, a true open court for a giant boss. Do not simply distribute random enemies by zone weight.
3. Build interior verticality. A playable chandelier/upper gallery has a different charm from endless roof bridges. Ground stairs must still work when stamina is empty; important height loops need more than one ascent/descent.
4. Keep boss cooperation distinct from a lasting alliance. The remote invitation must explain its target, accept/decline actions, and consequence. Do not silently change player allegiance from a boss prompt without a defined system.
5. Reward cards explain action consequences, category, currently owned state and confirmation. Mix of passive and consumable choices requires clear labels; it cannot be represented by three generic colored squares.
6. Cull survival, final qualification, ordinary death and final elimination are different emotional/game states. Their messages, sound, camera and score handling need explicit state transitions, tested by Claude.
7. Environmental charm is structural as well as decorative: support-bearing cathedral galleries, mortuary side lanes, carriage scale, working lamps, lower wet passages, pale flower gardens and extreme moon silhouette. Uniform Gothic texture on boxes cannot supply those differences.
8. The two visibly different final arenas argue for separating final rules from arena art. The match state machine should not hard-code one garden transform if later worlds/arenas are planned. This does not require implementing arena randomization now.

No exact attack wind-up, recovery, hitstop, projectile speed, HP drain, phase duration or input responsiveness was measured in this survey. Those remain unknown until dense sequences are inspected or are explicitly chosen as BloodLocks tuning values.
