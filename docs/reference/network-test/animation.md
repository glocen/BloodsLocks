## 5. What reads well (DESIGN notes for our animation work)

- **Clear holds:** the Light of Hayern's raised-arm hold (≈1 s) and the bite's grab both read at a glance, even from far away.
- **Big silhouettes:** the free hand raised for the Blood Blade, arms spread for a pledge: each action has one pose a player recognizes.
- **Fast contacts:** the sabre's active arc is only 2–3 frames; the weight comes from the target's instant flinch, the blood and the push, not from slow swings.
- **Planted power:** the beam roots the hero; the brace (feet wide) sells the recoil.
