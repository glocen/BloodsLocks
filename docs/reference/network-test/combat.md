## 2. Albert's kit

- **Armament** (52:09; WIKI): "Lorrik's Jeweled Saber & SMG", a pair of weapons inlaid with dark jewels: the sabre for melee, the submachine gun for ranged pressure.
- **The sabre:**
  - **Light chain:** two hits measured ≈0.6 s apart (23:10); trails and blood in [vfx.md](vfx.md).
  - **Strong and charged attacks** exist (MANUAL 3:32); not measured.
  - **Air attacks:** every attack also works in the air (MANUAL).
- **The SMG:**
  - **A 40-round magazine** ("40/40" at the lower left); the counter drops by 6–8 per burst (40 → 32 → 24 …).
  - **Reload** when empty (0/40); back to 40/40 later.
  - The rounds fan a little (spread), and a burst reads as one golden ray up close.
- **Skill 1, Blood Blade** (MANUAL 4:08; WIKI): fires a blade formed of the wielder's blood; hold to fire a stronger one.
  - **Uses:** 3 to start ("x3", "3/3"); enhancement raises it to 4 (33:03).
  - **Cooldown:** 12, lowered to 8 by enhancement (43:07).
  - **Attack power:** 67 → 73 → 80 through enhancements (48:13, 48:52).
- **Skill 2, Light of Hayern** (MANUAL 39:49; WIKI): raises the crystal on the gun's casing and shines a beam; hold for a continuous beam. The hero is planted while firing.
  - **Unlocks at Lv. 12** (a notice: "Required level achieved", 24:43, 39:36); enhancement can lower the requirement (31:18).
  - **Attack power:** 195 → 215 → 234 (or 195 → 253 in one offer) through enhancements (31:10, 31:18, 48:37).
  - **"Usage Cooldown 1 → 0.6"** in several offers (units unknown).
  - The two skills are switched with the pad (MANUAL 39:49); the skill emblem shows the other skill in a small inset.
- **Aspects** (passives; 52:09; WIKI): High Lorrik Blood (blood skills start with extra uses); Upriser (more stagger against Bloodsworn with high Virtue).
- **Healing:** Goldblood stakes (a count in the small red banner above the emblems). Driving one in restores HP; Goldblood drops from enemies refill them (MANUAL 7:54; "Gain Goldblood" over bodies, 45:20).

## 3. Defence

- **Guard** (MANUAL 9:21): a shield that blocks attacks from the front, also in the air. Repeated blocks break it and leave you open; it restores over time. A red translucent disc in front of the body (9:40); a translucent red sphere around a guarding rival (6:05); an orange-gold flash with sparks when a hit is blocked (14:43).
- **Howl** (MANUAL 9:36): guard + strong makes a gust of wind. Timed with an enemy's attack, it interrupts the attack and fills the enemy's stagger gauge greatly; it also deflects ranged attacks (not the strongest ones). A pale shockwave sphere (9:42, 33:56).
- **Parry:** no separate parry was identified in the survey (the WIKI and our earlier notes describe a perfect block; not confirmed here).
- **Dodge:** on the ground and in the air, with an afterimage (10:07, 24:02, 25:38). Its invincibility can't be measured from these frames.

## 4. Stagger and drinking blood

- **Stagger gauges** (the game's word; the owner calls it posture; MANUAL 9:34): attacks fill a gauge; full means a stagger and a big opening (the illustration shows the enemy kneeling).
  - **On enemies:** a thin gold line under the HP bar, growing from its centre (36:07.2, 44:50.2).
  - **On the player, the posture bar:** a thin red line whose yellow-gold segment grows from its centre, low in the middle of the screen, shown while it fills (36:07.2; confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-30). Stamina is the separate green bar under the HP bar at the top left.
- **Drink Blood** (MANUAL 9:57): on a staggered enemy (some are immune), it deals heavy damage and heals you. **Weaker foes are spellbound: they follow and obey their new master.**
  - **Thralls in play:** white-outlined humanoids and hounds fighting on the hero's side (36:15–36:42, 38:00–39:31).
  - **The bite's stages:** grab, bite, drain, release, burst (≈3.5 s in all; [vfx.md](vfx.md)).
- **Enhanced Drink Blood** is a Power of Blood (46:31): more damage from drinking.

## 5. Damage feedback

- **A running damage total** beside the target's bar: it grows hit by hit (148 → 222 → 328 at 3:32–3:41) and is the only damage number seen; no number pops per hit.
- **Great foes and trespassers** show their name and a long two-layer bar (HP over a gold stagger line) at the **top centre**, with the running total at its right (14:52, 16:13, 36:04).
- **Ordinary foes and rivals:** a thin bar over the head, in the world; rivals' names in red.
- **The player's HP bar** has a lag chip: fresh damage shows as a pale segment that drains after the hit (36:07.2).
- **Hit reactions:** a flinch (the body jerks) and a push on the same frame as the hit; heavy hits knock down; a staggered foe kneels.
- **Taking a heavy hit:** a red radial burst at the screen's centre and a camera shake (33:45).
- **Floor splatter** stays and accumulates over a fight.

## 6. Growth in a match

- **Levels** (MANUAL 9:09): XP from kills; a level raises attack and max HP. Lv. 1 → 20 over this match; the XP gained shows beside "Lv.".
- **Powers of Blood** (MANUAL 14:57): up to five held at once, swappable. Offered two at a time (or Virtue) at blood roses. Seen:

  | Power | Effect (paraphrased) |
  |---|---|
  | Plunging ATK Up | Stronger plunging attacks |
  | Self-heal upon Stake Use | Using a stake also heals over time |
  | Melee ATK Up +1 / Ranged ATK Up +1 | More melee / ranged damage |
  | Max Stamina Up +1 / Stamina Recovery Up +1 | More stamina / faster recovery |
  | Stagger DEF Up +1 | Harder to stagger |
  | Max HP Up | More HP |
  | Defeating Foes Restores HP +1 | Kills heal |
  | Enhanced Drink Blood +1 | More damage from drinking blood |
  | Milhüsa's Sword Technique (from a trespasser) | More attack after landing consecutive hits; less stamina for movement |

- **Skill enhancement** (MANUAL 31:12): blood crystals (from bloodsacs, or found) offer a choice of two boosts, or none: max uses, cooldown, attack power, level requirement.

## 7. The kin (companions)

- **Compacts** (MANUAL 30:20–30:27, 35:32–35:38): made at summoning circles at the foot of chains of light. Three kinds, shown by the crest: Ring, Blade and Eye (Eye kin are unusual and harder to command). A new compact breaks the old one and lowers the kin's level.
- **Summon or dismiss** through the Function Menu. A dead kin can't be summoned for a while; dismissed or out of combat, its HP restores.
- **Levels** (MANUAL 39:54): kin gain XP from kills and gold rings and level up to 5; a level raises attack and HP and sometimes adds attacks.
- **Seen:**
  - **Valentius the Unwise** (Eye): a huge bloated figure fused to a wheeled cart under a red canopy, a fanged mouth on the cart. He rams, lashes with a whip-like tongue, and breathes fire at higher levels. He fights in a white outline and rolls along the roofs with the hero.
  - **Baritone** (Ring): a gaunt singer (41:36–41:47).

## 9. PvP rules

- **Sword Virtue** only at ritual sites (MANUAL 18:38); PvP elsewhere gives nothing.
- **Co-op battles** against trespassers: Bloodsworn can't hurt each other inside (MANUAL 15:51).
- **Alliances** last until the final (MANUAL 42:02).
- **The final:** everyone fights; two lives each; alliances end.
