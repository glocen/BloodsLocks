# Match flow: from the hub to the outcome

How one session runs, from the hub through a full four-phase match to the outcome, as seen in the first full playthrough (the tutorial match "The Blood-Drenched Moon", 29:05–52:23, plus the earlier tutorial matches). **OBSERVED** unless labelled; **MANUAL** marks the game's own tutorial cards (paraphrased). Times are source positions. Screens are detailed in [ui.md](ui.md); our adaptation is in [takeaways.md](takeaways.md).

**Caution:** these are tutorial matches with four entrants, and tutorial cards may pause the clock. The timings below are what this footage shows, not the live game's rules.

## 2. The hub (House of Night)

- **Stations:**
  - **The Wheel of Blood** (a small dais under a violet window): opens the menu. The floor around it flares red when touched (12:21–12:27, 13:02–13:05).
  - **The withered book:** a travel list between hub rooms (Hall, Courtyard) with a preview picture (13:06–13:09).
  - **The dais** in the main hall: where the hunter appears and stands in menus (52:22).
- **Menu tabs** (52:09): Dusk Battle (matchmaking), Bloodsworn Selection (the class), Battle Remembrance (tutorials), Archive, System. Detailed in [ui.md](ui.md).
- **Arrival in the hub** (11:26–12:11): a curtain of vertical red light strands rains onto a floor circle, red mist rolls, the body forms lying down and rises.

## 3. Starting a match

1. **Loading** with the turning red emblem.
2. **The intro card:** the map's painted matte, "Dusk Battle" over the mode's name, and tall portrait cards sliding in one by one; the player's in colour, the rivals' dark (29:31–29:37).
3. **Sigil manifested** (when a sigil applies): a small modal naming the sigil and its icon, before the map (20:15–20:21).
4. **The intermission** (the start-area choice):
   - The info screen's map shows **one soft circle per region**, overlapping, with a pale rim; a region with a trespasser shows its icon inside (29:38).
   - **Focus** turns a circle's rim bright gold with a glow; the others stay pale silver (29:46–29:59).
   - The dial shows "Until end of intermission" with a **20 s countdown**; the last digits turn red.
   - The phase strip shows **how many entrants each phase admits** under its medallion (20:23).
   - **At zero:** the other circles fade, the chosen one stays gold, the screen dims (37:12–37:18), then **≈5 s of black**.
   - **MANUAL (29:44):** you start at a random point inside the chosen area; if too many choose one area, some are moved at random to another.
5. **Arrival:** the hunter forms on a **glowing red ground sigil** inside rising red strands; **"PHASE"** over the phase numeral's medallion holds for about 3 s (30:09–30:12, 37:24–37:26, 44:31–44:35).

## 4. The phases

- **MANUAL (29:39–29:42):** four phases. Phase I has no ritual sites: explore and grow. Ritual sites appear in phases II and III. A culling follows the ends of phases II and III (card 44:04). The final is a showdown.
- **Phase I** (30:09–36:45): great foes, trespassers, lantern corpses (Flame Virtue), kin summoning circles, bloodsacs (skill upgrades), blood roses (Powers of Blood), items. PvP gives no Virtue outside ritual sites.
- **Phases II and III:**
  - The objective reads "Explore the Lands of Dusk until a ritual site appears".
  - When the clock's hand reaches its **shining dot**, a **ritual site is established**: a gold notice across the upper middle (41:20, 47:22). From then the dial **counts down the seconds left in the phase: 135 s** here.
  - The site is a gold-washed region on the map; its pledge pillars show as icons and on the compass.
  - **At the site:**
    - swear to **blood pledge pillars** for Pledge Virtue (8–12 here); another Bloodsworn can overwrite your pledge and take it;
    - defeat Bloodsworn for **Sword Virtue** (6–12 here);
    - claim a **redstone** (more Sword Virtue) or a **goldstone** (more Pledge Virtue) from rock plinths; never both (MANUAL 41:32, 43:49).
  - **Leaving the site** after entering it hurts: "Left Ritual Site", then "Ritual Site Exit Damage" with a draining bar, while outside (42:24–43:30).
  - **"1 Minute Remaining"** shows once (35:45, 42:47, 48:38).
- **Phase lengths here:** phase I ≈6.6 min, phase II ≈6.4 min (the ritual window its last ≈2¼ min), phase III ≈5.1 min.

## 5. The clock (the top-right dial)

| State | What it shows |
|---|---|
| A phase running | A hand sweeping around a ring of ticks; a **red dot** on the rim marks the phase's end (MANUAL 30:13), a **shining dot** the ritual site's opening (MANUAL 37:27). |
| Ritual site open | The seconds left in the phase, as a number in the dial's centre (135 → 0). |
| Last minute of phase I | The seconds left (60 → 0). |
| Intermission | "Until end of intermission" and a 20 s countdown, its last digits red. |
| The final | No clock. |

## 6. The end of a phase

1. **Banner:** "PHASE END" over the phase's numeral medallion at the top centre.
2. **The band:** "You survived the culling" (phases I and II), or **"You were beckoned by the grail"** (phase III, for the finalists), in large warm serif on a full-width dark band. The world and the fight go on behind it for **≈6–8 s**.
3. **The breakdown (≈5 s):** the world darkens and the HUD goes; one row per Virtue category earned this phase (icon, count, name), a hairline, the **phase's total**, and the **match total in the big red medallion** at the bottom centre.
4. **Black (≈2 s)**, then the next intermission.

**The culling (MANUAL 44:04):** at the ends of phases II and III, the Bloodsworn without enough Virtue lose and don't reach the next phase. **Your Virtue number turns red while you're below the cut.** In the standings, a hunter below the cut shows dimmed and red (Bloodhand Jan at 44:07).
- **In this tutorial:** four entrants; nobody was culled after phase II; Jan (lowest) was culled after phase III; three reached the final.

## 7. The final

1. **The finalists card** (49:55–50:11): at the left the final's event card ("Moon Blood Bestowal": a crimson moon emblem and two lines of lore); at the right three tall portrait panels with each finalist's Virtue in a red medallion.
2. **The rules card** (MANUAL 49:59): fight until one remains; all alliances end; **two lives each**; extra HP from the Virtue earned in all previous phases.
3. **Black (≈5 s)**, then the arrival in the final's own arena with the **"GRAIL BATTLE"** banner (50:17).
4. **The final's HUD:** the top-left medallion shows **two candle-flame marks** (the lives) instead of the Virtue number; the HP bar is longer, its bonus part a lighter pink; no clock, no compass, no area name; the objective "Defeat all other Bloodsworn".
5. **Victory** (51:38–51:45): the camera tilts up to the blood moon, which swells into a crimson nebula with a gold ring; **"You are the Victor of this Fleeting Dusk"** on the band; a gold sigil glows on the winner's back. Then black.
6. **The outcome** (51:51–51:53): Outcome and Dusk Record tabs; a gold grail for the winner; the match's Virtue by category; "Return to the House of Night".

## 8. Virtue (the match score)

| Category | Earned by | Amounts seen |
|---|---|---|
| **Sword** | Defeating Bloodsworn at a ritual site (nothing outside one) | 6–12 |
| **Pledge** | Swearing to blood pledge pillars | 8–12 |
| **Repulsion** | Great foes and trespassers | 3–5 (great foes), 5–8 (trespassers) |
| **Flame** | Lighting the skull of a seated lantern corpse (first to arrive only) | 2 (small), 6 ("Large Flame") |
| **Other** | Virtue pickups dropped by kills, sigils, reward choices | 1 per pickup; "Virtue (3)" as a reward option |

- The total lives in the **red medallion at the top left**; each gain shows a **toast at the top centre** (category icon, amount, name).
- This match ended at 71: Sword 18, Pledge 12, Repulsion 20, Flame 19, Other 2.

## 9. Growth inside a match

- **Levels:** kills give XP (white wisps fly into the body); a level raises attack and max HP (MANUAL 9:09). The hero went from Lv. 1 to **Lv. 20** in this match. The XP gained shows as "+963" beside "Lv." at the top left.
- **Skills:** the second skill unlocks at a level (Light of Hayern at Lv. 12; 24:43, 39:36), announced by a notice at the right edge.
- **Powers of Blood:** a defeated great foe or Bloodsworn leaves a **blood rose**; taking it offers a choice of two powers (or Virtue). Up to **five** are held at once (MANUAL 14:57).
- **Skill enhancement:** bloodsacs drop **blood crystals**; each offers a choice of two boosts for the skills (more uses, shorter cooldown, more attack, a lower level requirement) or none (MANUAL 31:12).
- **Kin:** compacts at summoning circles (three kinds: Ring, Blade, Eye). Kin gain XP from kills and gold rings and level up to 5; a new compact replaces the old one and the kin's level drops (MANUAL 35:32–35:38).
- **Items:** up to six kinds carried (MANUAL 8:30): Fire Flask, Mirror Charm, Beckoning Black Cat, Goldblood Boluses, Spotted Blood Boluses, Sigil Perfume. **Goldblood stakes** heal and refill from Goldblood drops.

## 10. Death and revival (38:36–38:47)

1. The body breaks into pale vertical streaks and shreds.
2. If you were in a co-op battle: "Left co-op battle".
3. **"You Are Defeated"** on the band (≈4 s); the world goes on behind it.
4. The first time: the "Resurrection" card (MANUAL 38:42): you revive and keep fighting; choose a revival point on the map; no level or XP is lost; you revive with at least two stakes.
5. **The revival map:** the info screen with "Select revival point".
6. Arrival at the chosen point.

## 11. Co-op battles, travel, alliances, sigils

- **Co-op battles (trespassers, MANUAL 15:25, 15:51):** a trespasser fighting a Bloodsworn invites those nearby. A compact request shows low in the centre (accept or decline by holding); accepting transports you there in a white streak column; inside, Bloodsworn can't hurt each other; everyone engaged earns Virtue and a Power of Blood by their share; afterwards everyone returns to where they were (15:12–17:23).
- **Instant travel (MANUAL 17:51):** pick a travel point on the map; not right after a phase starts or a revival, not soon after another travel, not in battle. Leaving and arriving show a white streak column (17:57–18:05).
- **Alliances (MANUAL 42:02):** another Bloodsworn can petition you (a small "Alliance requested" notice; check or close); accepting forms an alliance that lasts until the final; you can also petition from the Function Menu or the info screen.
- **Sigils (MANUAL 20:15–21:16, 28:14–28:56):** personal goals bound to other Bloodsworn (a Sworn Enemy to defeat, an Old Friend, and others); you can sense a counterpart nearby; lock-on shows their role over their HP gauge; resolving one earns Virtue.
- **Observation (MANUAL 45:35):** lock on to another Bloodsworn through the Function Menu to see their details.
