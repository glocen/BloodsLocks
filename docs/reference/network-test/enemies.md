# Enemies, bosses and companions

Every creature and opponent seen in the first full playthrough (0:00–52:23), and where they stand in the world. **OBSERVED** unless labelled; **MANUAL** marks tutorial cards (paraphrased). The names are theirs: never use them, their designs or their silhouettes 1:1 (AGENTS.md); ours are designed from the roles below.

## 4. Objects that fight back

- **Bloodsacs:** floating glossy crimson spheres with a small HP bar. Shoot or cut them; they burst in a red flood and drop **blood crystals** (skill enhancements) (30:52–31:11, 42:49–43:08, 47:58, 48:28–48:31).

## 6. Thralls

**Weaker foes bitten by the hero follow and obey him** (MANUAL 9:57). They fight in a thin pale outline: humanoids with blades and hounds (36:15–36:42, 38:00–39:31).

## 7. Rival Bloodsworn

Bloodhand Jan, Trang Lanh, Senator Samir Patres and Sniper Layla in this playthrough; Zork (a diver's suit, a speargun) in the class list. Their kits are in [combat.md](combat.md) §8. A rival's name shows in red over their head, with a red HP bar and a gold stagger line.

## 8. Where encounters sit

- **Packs** hold stairs, landings, terraces and the nave's lanes; torch mobs gather around fire cages in plazas.
- **Brutes** stand on terraces and broad streets with room to swing.
- **Great foes** take open courts, bridges and lake shallows.
- **Trespassers** are fought in their own fields (the swamp) or in wide streets.
- **Bloodsacs** float in halls, on stairs and by windows; **lantern corpses** sit in streets and chapels, guarded by packs.
- **Kin circles** stand on streets and bridges, visible from afar by their chains of light.
