# Scenery: places, light and time of day

Every place in the first full playthrough (0:00–52:23), by phase and time of day. **OBSERVED** throughout unless labelled; times are source positions; full frames are in `build/reference/network-test/frames/scenery/` (local). For how we use this, see [takeaways.md](takeaways.md). Our worlds take the structure and the mood, never a place 1:1 (AGENTS.md).

## 1. The look in brief

- **Dark architecture against a bright, coloured sky.** Gothic stone and slate read as layered dark silhouettes; the sky carries the colour of the phase. Distance dissolves into coloured haze (strong aerial perspective).
- **Three kinds of light in every scene:**
  - a cool ambient (violet, blue-grey or teal);
  - warm practicals at human scale (candles, gas lamps, braziers, fire);
  - a cold key from the sky, moon or tall windows.
- **Red is reserved** for blood, rituals and the moon. Gold marks objectives (pledge pillars, kin circles, Virtue).
- **The world is busy but the lanes are clear:** white dust sheets, bodies, tables, candle clusters, carts and urns fill rooms and plazas, while the routes stay readable.

## 3. The match map's regions

The map shown in the info screen has four regions (14:06, 20:23): Euthania Cathedral, City Streets, Leethi Greatbridge and Nether Gate Lake. Each is a district of one connected city on a hill, with real interiors and several height layers.

### Euthania Cathedral

- **Approach:** a terrace plaza of brick and stone with gas lamps and bare trees (22:13–22:23), then a **grand twin stair** to a pointed gothic portal with carved wood doors, a gilded tympanum and **rows of candles in niches** along the façade (18:00–18:11, 22:36–22:40).
- **The nave:** grey-violet light, stone saints in niches, tall candle stands, **pews and white dust sheets everywhere**, bodies on the floor, red banners (18:12–18:17, 22:48–22:59).
  - **The ritual dais** at its centre (18:24–18:31): a raised octagon ringed with melted candles on sand, a bier under a white cloth, a carved wooden triangular candelabrum (a gilded pyramid of pipes in the night version, 45:16–45:27), and a red glyph ring on the floor around it at night.
  - **Light:** cool grey-violet ambient, cold white shafts from tall windows, warm candle pools at floor level; routes read by the candles and windows (23:13–23:59).
- **Chapels and halls:** side chapels with candle clusters standing on the floor, carved choir screens, a gilded altarpiece, a confessional; a black-and-white checker floor (23:13–23:23); halls with long tables (23:24–23:35); a library hall with shelves and urns (24:48); a polished floor reflecting the candles (24:33).
- **Verticality inside:** upper galleries with balustrades and pointed arches, a rose window (24:12–24:23); a **great chandelier you can stand on** (25:00–25:03); ledge grabs on balustrades (25:05); a grand double staircase with gold inlay on its risers (25:06–25:11, 48:15).
- **The roof plaza** (25:12–25:23 at dusk, 48:44–49:11 at night): a long paved walkway between **spiky black gothic pinnacles**, lined with **braziers burning in stone urns** and iron lamp posts, ending at a **gilded dome chapel** (gold tracery, blue stained glass). Night version (full frame 48:46): glossy violet pavers, a crimson sky behind the dome, steel-blue mountains below. A second terrace at the roof's edge looks over blue mountains and mist (48:53–48:56).
- **Ochre courts:** a broad court of warm **ochre sandstone** façades with gothic arches and braziers (25:24–25:31).
- **The lily garden:** dense beds of **white lilies** either side of a paved axis toward a chapel with a steep gable and a gold rose at its peak; a white marble statue of a hooded woman with candles (25:44–25:53). At night the far end is a **gilded shrine** with a pledge pillar (49:29–49:37).
- **The cliff terrace:** the garden ends at a balustrade over a **sea of mist and layered mountains** in orange sun-glow, black spires at its edges (25:54–26:06).

### City Streets

- **Rooftops:** a complete walkable layer of steep slate and tile roofs, ridges, flat ledges and **chimneys pouring black smoke columns** into the sky (21:06–21:35, 38:49–39:08). The fast route through the district.
- **Streets and plazas:** lamplit cobbled streets, stone stairs between tall brick walls with balustrades (22:05–22:12), a **tiered stone fountain** in a plaza with bare black trees (22:00–22:03), an arched bridge over a canal (21:36–21:47), a plaza with an **iron fire cage** brazier (32:16), a statue on a column (39:09).
- **The canals below:** a dark underpass hung with **white cloth like veils**, an arch with an iron grate, wet stone (26:41–26:59).
- **A misty grove** with an abandoned black coach under trees and gas lamps (28:12).
- **Composed views:** a bare tree black against a blazing orange-gold sky through an arch (22:04); a long viaduct over a misty gorge with a cliff-top city beyond (27:48–27:59).
- **Landmark:** the cathedral's mass and spires are always on the skyline.

### Leethi Greatbridge

- **The bridge road:** a long, broad road with iron railings toward a walled cliff city, smoke rising (26:24–26:29); arcades and a round crenellated tower (28:00–28:11). You can **leap off it** and fall a long way into the city below (26:30).
- **The cathedral's front:** the main façade with a rose window, black spires and gas lamps under a red sky (26:14–26:23).
- **A plaza of black hearse-like carriages** (glass panels, brass lanterns, spoked wheels) between small spired kiosks (43:31–43:35).
- **The ritual site's edge** at twilight: red vertical light columns (redstone plinths) around a dais (43:36–43:55).

### Nether Gate Lake

- **The lake:** blue-green water under a stone bridge, ivy and moss on the walls, warm lamps (14:24–14:35); a gothic castle mass, dark cliffs, a blood-red glow in the sky (13:47).
- **The castle bridge:** a tall arched stone bridge over a misty gorge, round towers, a cliff of pink-grey rock (41:06–41:19).
- **Ruins and forest:** a ruined castle terrace with cracked flagstones and broken parapets (40:24–40:34); a rocky forest path; a hilltop of pines over a sea of mist (40:35–40:41); a plank walkway up a cliff with red flowers in the grass (41:29); a twilight park with an iron-railed stone bridge and lamps (39:56–40:11).
- **The shore:** snow on the ground, a long moonlit stair, tower walls to climb (42:29–43:27).

### The final arena (the "Grail Battle")

Its own place, not part of the match map (50:17–51:45; full frames 50:18, 51:42):
- a broad **field of white lilies** crossed by paved paths, edged by balustrades with stone urns;
- a **leaning gothic clock tower** on one side, bare black trees, a gothic ruin;
- the **blood moon** filling the top of the sky, dripping a thin stream of blood; a crimson sky;
- everything tinted pink and red by the moon. Petals scatter as bodies pass.

## 4. Tutorial spaces and the hub

- **The opening film** (0:37–2:42): a dim room in cold blue-teal, moonlight shafts with dust, deep shadow; the only saturated colour is red (a sleeve, the blood sigil).
- **The first vista** (2:58–3:12): a gothic hill-city of spires and domes floating above a sea of cloud; a gold-amber horizon under a violet-grey sky; a **red eclipse** (a dark disc with a crimson corona) top right; cold blue rock and green grass in front.
- **Cliff paths and a misty glen** (3:12–3:48): leaning gravestones, gnarled roots, white flowers, a cold blue stream; ivy to climb.
- **A graveyard chapel** with warm doorways (4:00–4:33); **the tutorial cathedral** with a red fresco in its dome (4:37–5:04).
- **The burning wood and village** (8:24–10:31): snowy blue-grey ground against walls of orange-red fire, embers, a red haze; torches, barrels, a stone well; a whole valley burning at the map's edge.
- **The Lady of the Bog's swamp** (the co-op field, 15:48–17:20): grey-green fog, drowned ruins, gothic arches, dead pines, cool white sky, warm amber light through the trees on one side.
- **The hub (the House of Night)** (11:12–13:20):
  - a tall gothic hall of **carved dark-red wood** (tracery screens, balconies, a wooden staircase with turned posts), a huge dark bronze chandelier, purple-blue light from high windows in hazy shafts, floor mist;
  - a giant open book with flesh-like pages on a lectern before a carved screen, with a painted sigil diagram and tall red candles;
  - a chamber with glass-front bookcases, sconces and a tall **violet stained-glass window**; a polished dark floor with faint red sigil lines;
  - the **raised octagonal dais** with a floor circle, where the hero stands in menus.

## 5. Props and set dressing

- **Light sources:** candle clusters standing on floors, tall candle stands, wrought-iron candle trees, gas lamps on iron posts, sconces, braziers in stone urns, iron fire cages, torches carried by enemies, a burning chandelier.
- **Furniture and debris:** pews, white dust sheets over everything, biers, long tables, bookcases, urns and jars, choir screens, confessionals.
- **Bodies:** robed corpses on floors and stairs; seated corpses in chairs (the lantern corpses).
- **Streets:** carts and black carriages, a fountain, statues on columns, iron railings and fences, balustrades with urns, kiosks with spires.
- **Nature:** white lilies in dense beds, bare black trees, dead pines, ivy with red leaves, red flowers in grass, snow.
- **Blood:** red splatter and stains stay on floors after fights and accumulate (19:12–19:17, 32:24–32:59, 46:54–47:21).

## 6. Verticality and routes

- **Layers:** canals and underpasses; streets and plazas; stairs, terraces and bridges; interior galleries and chandeliers; roofs and roof plazas.
- **Ways up:** stairs (often grand and wide), ivy and grips, ledge grabs on balustrades, wall climbing on towers (43:17–43:27), jumps from roof to roof.
- **Ways down:** long free falls (from the Greatbridge into the city, 26:30); drops through floor openings onto the dais (24:24).
- **Landmarks:** the cathedral dominates every skyline; the roof plaza's dome chapel, the Greatbridge and the castle bridge are visible from far away.
- **Vistas at the edges:** balustrades open onto seas of mist and layered mountains, framed by spires.
