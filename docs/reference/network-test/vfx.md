# VFX: every effect, broken into its layers

> **ROBLOX TERMS OF USE FIRST: NO GORE, EVER** (AGENTS.md rule 4; owner, 2026-10-31). The reference's blood effects below are recorded as observation only and are **never reproduced**. Our hits show as the body flashing red with a white outline. In doubt, ask the owner.

Every effect seen in the first full playthrough (0:00–52:23). The key ones are **measured** from dense bursts (frame rate given) and full frames; the rest are described from the 1-second survey. **OBSERVED** unless labelled. Our own effects are judged against these breakdowns, but the look stays our own (the art direction in the [README](README.md)); what we change is in section 12 and [takeaways.md](takeaways.md).

## 1. The visual language

- **Blood has two layers:**
  - **sharp, saturated crimson droplet shapes for the cut:** countless droplets and thin strings massed into bold forms (cones for sabre hits, a crescent for the Blood Blade, a fountain and a fan for the bite). Lit liquid red with darker red inside; not glowing, not near-black. Each lives a fraction of a second, then thins and fades;
  - **dark wine-red mist masses** for the weight of big hits (40:22).
- **White is magic and passage:** travel and arrival columns, deaths, XP wisps, the howl's shockwave, the dodge's afterimage, the Light of Hayern.
- **Gold is objectives and rites:** pledge pillars, kin circles, Virtue toasts, the sigil on the back after a pledge or a victory.
- **Violet** marks flame-of-the-dead objects (lantern corpses) and some deaths; **pink-red** the kin compact; **blue-violet** one rival's (Trang Lanh's) attacks.
- **Shapes are thick and readable:** big crescents, wide fans, columns, rings. Little relies on thin lines or glow alone. Combat effects are short (0.1–0.6 s); ritual effects linger.

## 2. Player attacks

**The sabre M1, measured** (cropped burst at 30 fps, 23:10.2–23:11.2):
- **The trail is a motion smear, not a coloured ribbon.** During the fast part of the swing (2–3 frames, ≈0.07–0.1 s) a **pale grey-white translucent wedge** follows the blade, two or three blade-widths wide, like heavy motion blur (23:10.233, 23:10.833). No glow; nothing lingers after the swing.
- **The blood hit is a sharp spray of droplets:**
  - at contact (≈0.07 s after the fast arc starts), **saturated crimson droplets and strings** burst from the point of impact, thrown **along the cut's direction** in a cone of about 60–90°;
  - the spray grows for ≈0.15 s (a dense clump of small droplets, thin strings and specks, bright red highlights, darker red in the middle);
  - it spreads, thins and fades by ≈0.4–0.5 s (23:10.300 → 23:10.700; again 23:10.867 → 23:11.167);
  - a red stain lingers on the target's body for a moment.
- **The target reacts on the same frame:** pushed back, its body jerks up (knockback and flinch).
- **Floor splatter:** dark-red decals stay where blood fell, and pile up over a fight.
- **Rhythm:** two hits about 0.6 s apart (contacts at ≈23:10.30 and ≈23:10.88).
- **The trail takes the scene's light** in other frames: a thin silver arc (9:30, 21:22), a broad pale crescent (21:41), a long thin white arc (24:04), gold-white or orange-gold in firelight (32:41, 34:12). At 42:42 and 47:18 **gold-orange spiral arcs** wrap the hero during a slash (likely a Power of Blood's effect). At 16:07 red light streaks follow the blade (a blood-infused slash; cause unknown).

**The SMG burst, measured** (cropped burst at 20 fps, 40:18.4–40:19.55; full frame 19:23.2):
- **No wind-up** on the gun.
- **The rounds:** at 40:19.35 **five or six thick, bright orange-gold streaks** appear at once, from the muzzle straight out of frame, nearly parallel and **fanned a little** (the spread). Each has a white-hot core and a saturated orange glow, as thick as a finger at 1080p.
- **No visible travel:** the streaks are at full length on their first frame, like hitscan rays. They thin and fade in ≈0.15–0.2 s.
- **The muzzle** (19:23.2): a big **orange-white bloom** that swallows the hand; up close the burst reads as **one continuous golden ray**; **brass casings** spin out.
- **Other frames:** a hot yellow-orange star flash with sparks (6:10); the muzzle lights dark places (26:49).

**Other hit effects:**
- A **red X-shaped burst** of blood streaks on a heavy or stagger hit (36:18).
- A **red radial burst at the screen's centre** (jagged red spikes from a dark core; 33:45): a hit on the player or a parry; the camera shakes.
- **Orange sparks** when shots hit armour or bone (6:12).
- A **pale grey swirl of dust** wraps a struck foe (6:14), and a dusty pinkish burst of smoke (9:50).

## 3. Skills

**Blood Blade, measured** (cropped bursts: 20 fps at 28:28.5–28:29.65; 10 fps at 28:32.4–28:35.3; the card 4:09–4:18):
- **Wind-up:** the hero raises his free hand toward the target (≈0.3–0.5 s); a small dark-red shape gathers in the palm.
- **The blade:** a **huge crescent of liquid blood**, three to four times the hero's height, thrown from the raised hand. **Dense saturated crimson droplets and darker streaks**, translucent at its thin parts, with a **sharp leading curve and ragged trailing edges breaking into droplets**. Side-on it reads as a long thick diagonal stroke (28:29.45–28:29.65); from behind, as a full sweeping crescent (28:33.0–28:33.1).
- **Flight:** it flies away along its arc and **thins into a long red streak within ≈0.5 s** (28:33.2–28:33.5). No glow, no light flash.
- **On contact:** the target is shoved back. At point-blank range a **huge cloud of dark wine-red mist** engulfs the target, with thin vertical red streaks rising at the hero's feet (40:22).
- **The card illustration** (4:09–4:18) shows the charged version as a massive crimson wave curling around the hero.

**Light of Hayern, measured in play** (burst at 10 fps, 48:31.6–48:35.7; full frame 48:33.3; also 51:01):
1. **Raise (≈0.2 s):** the gun arm goes straight up overhead, the body upright, the free arm out for balance.
2. **Charge (≈1.1 s):** at the gun's crystal, a **brilliant white eight-point star flare** with **long thin light streaks** reaching across the screen (two long diagonal rays and short cross rays, like a lens flare), a cold blue-white tint, a few white glints. The star pulses a little; the arm holds still.
3. **Aim (≈0.2 s):** the gun comes down and forward to the target; the body braces, feet wide.
4. **Fire:** a **big white-violet burst** at the muzzle, a sphere of white light with radial streaks, as wide as his torso.
5. **The beam (at least 1.1 s):** a **thin, perfectly straight, brilliant white ray** from the gun to the target, with **white spiral rings swirling around its root** (two or three concentric rings that fade after ≈0.3 s) and small sparks at the gun. He stays planted while it fires.
- **The card illustration** (39:51.6): arms flung wide, the gun arm straight at the target, the body leaning back from the recoil; pale cyan-white ribbons spiral around him and the beam's root, with glittering white motes; a white glare at the target.
- **Palette:** pure white with a cold blue-violet or cyan tint. No gold, no red.

**Drink Blood (the bite), measured** (10 fps, 28:43.4–28:47.0; also 34:30, 46:54):
1. **Grab (≈0.5 s):** he steps in and seizes the staggered foe from the front; she arches back, arms flung wide; her hat falls.
2. **Bite (≈0.4 s):** his head to her neck; a **narrow fountain of bright crimson droplets shoots straight up** from the bite, about a head and a half high, then sinks into a small red cloud.
3. **Drain (≈1.5 s):** he holds her; **tiny white glittering specks** swirl around his back (the heal) while thin red strings of blood fly up from the bite.
4. **Release:** he turns away; **pale white smoke ribbons whip in loops** around both bodies.
5. **The burst (≈0.6 s):** a **huge crimson fan of blood droplets** bursts from the victim, up and away, two to three times her height, dense and streaked (with a few specks of her own colour inside), while she staggers back and falls.

Others: a burst of white-grey smoke and shreds (39:32); **silver-white smoke ribbons and spikes bursting upward**, with a blue glowing rose left at the feet (45:09.6).
- **Trespassers:** a spray of water and pale ash where the boss stood (17:04).
- **Bloodsworn (PvP):**
  1. a **gold cross-sigil flashes on the loser's chest**, black tendrils whip out of the body (19:18);
  2. the body becomes **pale blue-white ghostly wisps** that sink and dissolve at the floor, or kneels in a violet glow with wisps rising (34:48);

- **Sabre M1:** our trail is now a short pale motion smear (`Vfx/SlashTrail`, 2026-10-31), and the hit is the body's red flash with a white outline (no blood effect: AGENTS.md rule 4). The target flinches and is pushed on the same frame.
- **SMG:** our one thin pale tracer flying at 700 studs/s doesn't match. The reference shows **several thick, saturated orange-gold rays at once**, fanned by the spread, fading in ≈0.2 s, with a big orange-white muzzle bloom and brass casings.
- **Blood Blade:** a huge crescent thrown from a raised free hand, thinning into a streak. Ours will be a stylized crimson energy crescent (light, sparks, mist; never liquid); ask the owner about its look first.
- **The Light Beam:** our brief's "warm golden light" doesn't match. The reference is **cold white**: raise overhead, star flare, aim, white burst, a thin straight white ray with swirl rings at its root, planted while firing.
