# Manor exterior — geometry review direction

Owner: model the manor in Blender, including the whole structure where useful; UI comes after a coherent manor art standard. Two usable storeys, red runners, dark natural timber, yard for training. No budget. Lighting is explicitly unfinished and unapproved.

## Composition and measured fit

- Centre: existing 120-stud hall footprint with octagonal wall envelope, top 78.66 studs. Broad hipped slate roof with clipped crown and one entrance-facing cross gable, avoiding a flat fortress silhouette.
- Wings: outer extents X = ±142.5, Z = ±21.75 relative to hall. Roof envelope ~69 studs; new pitched roofs give these inhabited rooms a domestic profile. Gallery surface remains 31.9125 studs.
- South entrance: layered roof shelter, open route framed by paired columns; existing spawn route remains traversable. Main visual sequence is still entry → Blood Table → rear doorway → practice yard → bell tower.
- Material roles: dark masonry fields, lighter dressed limestone around openings and at structural transitions, varied blue-gray slate courses, aged lead flashings, black iron and brown oak bargeboards. Wine-colored glazing is subordinate to the architecture.
- Detail: deep window reveals, connected tracery, overlapping slate courses, corbelled chimney caps, open pot rims, recessed dormer glazing. Add weathering only where runoff, joints or smoke explain it.

## Review before integration

Read the silhouette from a south three-quarter view, from the yard, and at the entrance at R6 camera height. Use neutral inspection lights first. Check roof intersections, window alignment, actual apertures, stone-frame depth, chimney seating and export normals. Central hall walls in the first Blender review are only fit guides; Studio's carved wall openings remain authoritative until a complete custom replacement passes review.

The Blender FBX importer produced a 100× scale factor for the candle kit. Normalize against known dimensions after manor import too. Export separate semantic mesh modules; keep editable source masters and preserve gameplay paths/zone tags.
